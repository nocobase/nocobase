# What is NocoBase 3?

**Let software keep pace with your business ambitions.**

NocoBase 3 is a foundation for building enterprise business systems with an AI Agent. It brings together application source code, reusable capabilities, and development guidance so you and your AI Agent can build on an existing foundation and focus development on business value.

**Our goal is to make NocoBase 3 a foundation for building, transforming, and continuously evolving business systems of every kind.** From a departmental tool to a complex system spanning teams, from a new idea to established software, we aim to provide a shared development foundation that turns business expertise more directly into software capabilities.

## Why choose NocoBase 3?

### Reuse capabilities and build lasting business value

Authentication, authorization, and notifications can provide a shared foundation for different business modules. Build customers, orders, projects, and processes on that foundation, making each development effort a starting point for the next extension. As your application grows, so does the business functionality your team can reuse.

### Make an AI Agent a partner in building your system

An AI Agent can read project files, edit code, and execute commands. NocoBase provides application structure, development conventions, and capability guidance, helping it turn requirements into connected pages, APIs, and data models. Bring your business goals, work with your AI Agent to implement complete features, and improve them through use.

### Own the source and shape the system's future

Application source code lives in your project. Your team can continue developing its interface, rules, and features. Manage versions, deploy the application, and let developers and AI Agents collaborate as the system evolves with your business.

## From a business idea to an evolving system

You bring business knowledge, your AI Agent turns requirements into code, and NocoBase supplies reusable capabilities. Together, they support a continuous cycle of requirements, implementation, feedback, and iteration within the same project.

## What system would you build?

**Start with the business system you want to create.** These are the application directions this foundation aims to support:

| Business area                       | Goals to design around                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer and commercial operations  | Connect customers, opportunities, contracts, and orders into a complete business process                      |
| Enterprise operations and resources | Organize cross-team data and processes around procurement, inventory, and assets                              |
| Project and team collaboration      | Connect projects, tasks, resources, and approvals around shared goals                                         |
| Industry-specific systems           | Shape data, interfaces, and processes around your industry's rules                                            |
| Existing system transformation      | Start with a module that needs change, plan business restructuring and integrations, and evolve incrementally |

The order tutorial demonstrates one concrete path: start with order entry, then add role permissions, supervisor approval, and result notifications to connect the business into a system.

![Order details and business actions in the tutorial application; interface shown in Chinese.](https://static-docs.nocobase.com/nb3-docs-20260916-tutorial-detail.png)

Use that example as a starting point to imagine your own modules, collaboration patterns, and complete application.
