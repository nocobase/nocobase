# AI knowledge bases overview

A knowledge base organizes articles, files and business instructions into maintainable, searchable company material. Employees can read it directly or have an AI employee retrieve accessible content to support an answer.

## Available capabilities

| Capability | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Organize and import material](./organize.md) | Spaces, folders, articles and files for different purposes. |
| [Read and search](./search.md) | Read source material, find content and connect semantic retrieval as needed. |
| [Knowledge-based answers](./answers.md) | Use company material in answers and link to sources. |
| [Access and collaboration](./access.md) | Assign read, propose, edit and manage permissions. |
| [Versions and processing status](./maintenance.md) | Keep versions and view file parsing and indexing status. |

The application composes the knowledge center's layout and entry points. Permissions, history and editing belong beside the relevant material rather than requiring a shared settings page.

## Use knowledge in your business

Northstar's material includes delivery requirements, temperature deviations and customer service procedures. Operations maintains the procedures, dispatchers read them, and the assistant answers questions with source links.

```text
Connect a knowledge center to Northstar for transport procedures, customer service material and driver handbooks.
Provide folders, articles, files, reading and search, with role-based access.
Let the Transport assistant answer from transport procedures and return document and passage links.
Connect the required model service, retrieval method and index while keeping sources clear in the employee interface.
```

Start with [knowledge-based answers](./answers.md). Basic reading and maintenance can be used independently. AI answers require an available model; semantic retrieval also requires its model and index integration.
