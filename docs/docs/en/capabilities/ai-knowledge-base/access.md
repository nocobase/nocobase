# Access and collaboration

Knowledge spaces, folders, and documents can grant read, propose, edit, and manage access. Readers consult content, contributors propose changes, editors maintain content and review proposals, and managers can also change access. Documents can inherit access from their space or parent folder, or allow only specified members.

## Example: maintain transport guidelines together

Northstar's operations administrators manage the transport guidelines space, while operations editors maintain its content. Dispatchers need to read guidelines and propose changes for editors to review. Financial materials are available only to authorized members.

```text
Assign access and collaboration permissions by responsibility in Northstar's knowledge center.
Operations administrators can manage transport guidelines, operations editors can edit and review proposals, and dispatchers can read and propose changes.
Show permissions and proposals beside documents. Allow only specified members to access financial materials; exclude dispatchers.
```

## See the result

Open **Temperature-controlled transport guidelines** to see each role's access. Dispatcher Emma proposes adding temperature deviation recording requirements. An operations editor can accept or reject the proposal; a pending proposal does not change the document directly. Financial materials use access limited to specified members, so dispatchers cannot access their content.

![Role access, a change proposal, and restricted financial materials](../assets/northstar-knowledge-access.png)

**Employees read and collaborate within their responsibilities, changes go through review, and restricted content stays within its authorized audience.**

:::tip Important

Space managers retain management access even when a document is limited to specified members. AI knowledge use must also respect access. Permission to propose changes does not grant direct editing or proposal review.

:::
