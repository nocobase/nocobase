Make phone layouts part of every application page from the first delivery: the application development Skill adds a phone experience guide (first screen, list cards, compact filters, one-row tabs, forms, overlays, touch and phone verification), replaces the old "the toolbar wraps and the table scrolls" acceptance rule, and updates its workflow, templates, examples and screenshot tool to match. The templates' `DataTable` gains `renderCard` to show rows as cards below 768px and a `DataTableSortMenu` for sorting without column headers.
