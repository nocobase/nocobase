Add My agents: talk in Studio with the coding agent on your own computer (Codex, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, OpenCode and more, on macOS, Linux or Windows), connected once with `nb-studio acp service install` and online whenever the computer is on. A page with no computer connected yet walks you through connecting it, and suggests using the strongest model you can. The agent analyzes and plans with you, and records the development you agree on as issues for the project manager, who assigns agents and moves them along once you start them; very small changes, such as a typo, it makes itself. It works with full access in the folder you choose by browsing your computer, for the project you choose, and creates and finds Studio issues through Studio's tools, in that project unless you name another; mention an issue in a message with `#`. The agent treats the session's issues as the tasks you mean, learns what changed on them since your last message, and can tell how each is going: who works on it, what it waits for, its pull request. The issues a session creates wait in backlog and show in the conversation, where you start the ones you keep in one step, and in the session list with their project and status. When your agent waits for your permission, replies or stops with an error while you are not looking at the session, your inbox tells you, one item per session that opens it, and the item settles once you have seen the session.
