Bind a conversation to the first runner that takes one of its runs: later runs wait for that runner, `ConversationSummary.runner` reports whether it is online, and `POST /api/agents/conversations/{conversationId}/switchRunner` lets the owner move on to another runner, which starts a fresh session with the history. A session whose working directory a runner no longer reports starts fresh as well. Conversations can be tied to records of kinds the application registers (`conversations.links`, `GET|POST|DELETE /api/agents/conversations/{conversationId}/links`, `linkKind` and `linkTargetId` on the conversation list), and every turn lists them as linked work; `conversations.rules.provide` may add `turn` sections, and `conversations.deliver` wakes a Runner conversation with `news.wake`. Conversations keep their owner's read position and what waits for them (`seenSeq`, `needsYou`, `POST …/markSeen`, `GET /api/agents/conversationAttention`, the `conversation.attention` event and the run command `conversation ask-owner`). Adds the `202611010001_ag_conversation_runner_attention` and `202611010002_ag_create_conversation_links` migrations.
