Add "My follow-ups" (`/follow-ups`), right above My issues in the sidebar and behind the same page grant. The left column keeps up to five issues the person follows, each with what it waits for now (or what it needs from them) and how many comments and changes others made since they last looked; until they choose, it shows their blocked and in-review issues. The middle column shows one issue live: its status, pull request with checks and conflicts, the "Now" and "Next" lines, what it needs from the person, and its activity told as hand-overs, replies, reviews, pull request and status events rather than raw comments, long comments shortened to their first line with the rest on request. The right column lists the issues the person owns, filtered by several statuses at once (In review when the page opens) and by title or key, each saying what it needs from them; a star follows or stops following an issue. `GET /api/followUps` (`follow-up list`) answers the overview, `PUT /api/followUps/following` replaces the followed issues and `POST /api/followUps/issues/{issueId}/markSeen` records a look; both are kept as the person's own preferences, so nothing needs migrating.
