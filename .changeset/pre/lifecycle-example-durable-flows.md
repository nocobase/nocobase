Add five durable flows to `@nocobase/app-plugin-lifecycle-example`: processes that stop to wait for systems outside the application and are moved on by them. An order is paid on a payment provider's hosted checkout and confirmed only by the provider's webhook, which replays when it is delivered twice, is accepted when it overtakes the checkout's own effect, and is refunded when it arrives after the order was cancelled or expired; an export from a vendor without webhooks is polled by a trigger-driven self-transition until a deadline written on the record; a flash-sale purchase reserves stock and then charges the card, and undoes the reservation by a compensating step of its own when the charge is refused, stopping for a person when the compensation keeps failing; a shipment waits for the payment and the warehouse in either order and then applies carrier events by when they happened rather than when they arrived; and a subscription is renewed by the sweep at its own period end and retried by a dunning trigger until it cancels. A sandbox in the plugin's own tables plays the payment provider, warehouse, vendor and carrier, idempotent by key, and sends signed webhooks to `POST /api/lifecycleExample/webhooks/{source}`, which takes no session and checks the signature instead; each flow has a page with a console to pay, decline, send, hold and redeliver those webhooks. Every page, and the record panel the help desk and expense reports already had, now draws the lifecycle as a Mermaid diagram with the record's current state filled and the states it passed through outlined. A new migration, `202610090001_lifecycle_example_create_durable_flows`, creates the five record tables and the sandbox's two. The plugin now declares `@nocobase/markdown-mermaid` as a peer dependency, which the examples template provides. Its pages no longer poll every few seconds: the provider publishes which record changed on the realtime topic `lifecycle-example:changes`, and a page reloads when told, looking again on its own only while one of the record's effect runs is still queued or running.
