Let native online agent conversations choose a thinking level (the AI SDK's seven reasoning levels: provider default, none, minimal, low, medium, high or extra high) for any online chat model, passed to the provider through the AI SDK, with a notice in the conversation when the model reports it does not support the level, and a thinking level menu beside the model choice in the Examples chat and the UI Library agent-chat block.
