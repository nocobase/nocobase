Add `defineSystemSettingsRoutes()` and `definePersonalSettingsRoutes()` so plugins and applications can declare settings pages again. System settings configure the whole application and are mounted under `/settings` (`SYSTEM_SETTINGS_ROUTE_PATH`, `surface: 'systemSettings'`, `isSystemSettingsRoute()`); a page without its own `authz` admits unrestricted administrators only. Personal settings are where each signed-in person configures their own account and are mounted under `/me` (`PERSONAL_SETTINGS_ROUTE_PATH`, `surface: 'personalSettings'`, `isPersonalSettingsRoute()`); a page without its own `authz` admits every signed-in person. Both take the same definitions as `defineAppRoutes()` and join the one shared route tree with its authorization, breadcrumbs and lazy component loading. Settings categories are built in: System settings have Accounts and permissions, Workflows and automation, Messages, AI, Data and files, System and Other (`SYSTEM_SETTINGS_CATEGORIES`), and personal settings have Account, Preferences and Other (`PERSONAL_SETTINGS_CATEGORIES`), titled in English and Chinese by app-client's own namespace (`APP_CLIENT_NAMESPACE`, with `appClientLocales`), which every application registers. A page joins one with a bare `parent` such as `parent: SYSTEM_SETTINGS_CATEGORIES.system`; a plugin or application adds a category of its own with a root group declaring `category: true`, and every other top-level group declares `kind: 'module'`. The application renames, reorders or hides a category with `defineSettingsCategories()`. A page naming an unknown or hidden category is shown under Other with a development warning; a category is named `<surface>.<id>` so one package may declare the same id in both surfaces, and declares no path or breadcrumb, so a page's URL never depends on its category; a duplicate category in one surface, a category with a path, an undeclared top-level group, and nesting a route under a group of another surface fail registration with a message that names the fix. Registered settings routes report `kind` and `category`.
