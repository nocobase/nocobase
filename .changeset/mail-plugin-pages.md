Mail registers its own pages: **Mail** (`/mail`, with the unread count in the navigation), **Team mail** (`/mail/team`), **Personal settings › Messages › Mail accounts** (`/me/mail`) and **System settings › Messages › Mail** (`/settings/mail`), each replaceable by route name with `routeComponentOverrides`; `mail({ routes })` moves or hides the two navigation entries. `mail.oauthReturnUrl` now defaults to `/me/mail`. Team mail and Mail settings permissions are registered with the authorization plugin and can be granted on the role permission page, and every `/api/mail` route checks the same requirement its page declares. Applications extend the pages through `mailExtensionsToken`: `providerTypes`, `slots.messageAside`, `slots.composeToolbar`, `slots.workspaceActions`, `templateVariables` and `composer.onComplete`; the existing page props keep working. New `GET /api/mail/settings/overview`, member, account, result and time filters on `/settings/syncRuns` and `/settings/submissions`, and retry and cancel of other members' runs and sends under `/settings`. Finished sync runs and sends are deleted after `mail.logRetentionDays` (`MAIL_LOG_RETENTION_DAYS`, default 90, `0` keeps them), and a migration indexes both log tables by creation time. `/settings/submissions` no longer returns subjects, bodies or addresses. Mail Example drops its five pages and extends Mail's pages instead, with a **Set up demo mailboxes** workspace action.

**Permission upgrade, without a compatibility layer:** `page:mail.workspace/access` is unchanged. `page:mail.management/access` is replaced by `mail:mail.management/view` (read-only Team mail), plus `mail:mail.management/process` for marking read, starring and archiving in bulk. `page:mail.admin/access` is replaced by `settings:mail.admin/read` (read-only Mail settings), plus `settings:mail.admin/manage` for retrying and cancelling other members' syncs and sends. Former grants are not converted and no longer open any route: check the application's role seeds and any code that writes these grants directly, and grant the replacements.
