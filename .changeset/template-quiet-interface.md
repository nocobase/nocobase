Quieten the default interface: every border takes one faint line, the sidebar's own rules and the Appearance options included, and a new `--hover` grey marks hovered rows, buttons and cards, table headers, and the navigation's current and hovered entries, whose words and icon carry the primary color. Inputs, buttons, links, tabs, checkboxes, switches and radios share one light focus ring. Cards and overlays are framed by the general border, and overlays sit on a faint, blurred backdrop. The compact preset uses `--spacing` 0.225rem and `--radius` 0.5rem. Page titles are `text-2xl`. A badge has a small size, `data-size='sm'` (`size='sm'` on `StatusBadge`), for one beside a title or on a dense card. Line tabs run full width from the left over a 2px underline. Table rows take the `--hover` grey and keep the primitive's padding. Scrollbars are thin and appear only while a region scrolls or the pointer nears its edge. Whatever can be clicked shows the pointer cursor.

The navigation renders a top-level group with no page of its own as a labelled section with its entries always visible (`sections` on `NavigationMenu`), and its rows take the sidebar primitive's sizes in a sidebar 64 spacing units wide. An entry can show a count, such as unread items, passed by the App layout in `NavigationMenu`'s `badges` keyed by route name: a number on a round ground, or work under way in the primary color behind a pulsing dot, hidden at zero, "99+" above 99, in the entry's accessible name and tooltip, and a dot on the icon when the sidebar is collapsed. Home cards lift on hover, and the inbox list and its tabs are restyled. The Appearance popover's color modes and presets are centered again: their hidden radios no longer take a place in the row. The sidebar footer's tagline and the descriptions under the sign-in, registration and password pages' titles and the sign-in page's brand panel drop their full stops in Chinese.

Examples now carries everything Default has in its shell: the brand loading mark, dialog, confirmation, alert, toast, badge and table styles, `AlertDialog`, `StatusBadge` and the branded startup screen. Its example pages put the small badge on cards and beside words, keep the default one in table columns and page headers, and no longer size badges with their own utilities.
