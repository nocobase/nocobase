Roles and permissions set a role in two tabs. Menus choose what the role sees in the sidebar and are stored as page access on their own; Business objects set each object's actions and data scope once for every menu that uses it, with other actions following or narrowing View and scopes titled by their registered collections. Settings pages appear as objects with a warning on the actions that can grant permissions, the system administrator is shown as unrestricted, and a new Permission check tab shows what a user can open and do, with a searchable user picker. A role can be duplicated, and switching roles asks before discarding unsaved changes.
