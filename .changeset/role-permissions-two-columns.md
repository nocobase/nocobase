Roles and permissions use the two-column permission editor: the roles are listed beside the selected role's menu tree and the selected menu's actions. Turning on any action of a menu opens its pages, other actions follow or narrow the View scope, system settings are configured under Administration with their real actions and a warning on the ones that can grant permissions, data scopes are titled with their registered collections, the system administrator is shown as unrestricted, and a new Permission check tab shows what a user can open and do, with a searchable user picker. A role can be duplicated, and switching roles asks before discarding unsaved changes.
