Declare the templates' System settings with `defineSystemSettingsRoutes()` and switch the sidebar to a settings menu on `/settings` pages. The System settings entry now stands apart at the bottom of the business menu; on any settings page the sidebar shows Back, returning to the page the viewer left, followed by each category over the modules the viewer may open, in the mobile drawer as well. The settings home, Users, Roles and permissions (formerly Permission management), Database (Database Explorer) and Notifications are settings routes placed in the built-in categories — Accounts and permissions, Data and files, and Messages — with App Foundation in System, and settings pages contributed by plugins join the same menu. Add personal settings under `/me`, opened from the first item of the account menu: the built-in Account, Preferences and Other categories with a Language and appearance page, Back that keeps the page left while moving between system and personal settings, and the person's identity above the menu. Examples moves its settings pages under `/settings` (`/settings/users`, `/settings/roles`, `/settings/default-access`, `/settings/workflows`, `/settings/knowledge/indexing`) and redirects the former URLs, keeping the path, query and hash. Closing a default data scope form now returns to the list wherever it is mounted. The default template now registers Users and Permission management from the start, in the Accounts and permissions category; each module shows only to those its page grant admits.
