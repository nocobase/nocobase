Add `defineSystemSettingsRoutes()` and `definePersonalSettingsRoutes()` so plugins and applications can declare settings pages again. System settings configure the whole application and are mounted under `/settings` (`SYSTEM_SETTINGS_ROUTE_PATH`, `surface: 'systemSettings'`, `isSystemSettingsRoute()`); a page without its own `authz` admits unrestricted administrators only. Personal settings are where each signed-in person configures their own account and are mounted under `/me` (`PERSONAL_SETTINGS_ROUTE_PATH`, `surface: 'personalSettings'`, `isPersonalSettingsRoute()`); a page without its own `authz` admits every signed-in person. Both take the same definitions as `defineAppRoutes()` and join the one shared route tree with its authorization, breadcrumbs and lazy component loading. A group declared at the root of a settings contribution is a category shared across packages of the same surface, so a plugin places a page with a bare `parent` such as `parent: 'settingsSystem'`; a missing or duplicate category, and nesting a route under a group of another surface, fail registration with a message that names the fix.
