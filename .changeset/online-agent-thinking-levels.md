Let native online agent conversations choose a thinking level (default, off, minimal, low, medium, high, extra high or max) for any online chat model, passed to the provider through the AI SDK, with the provider's warnings about the level shown in the conversation, and a thinking level menu beside the model choice in the Examples chat.
