Apps are listed and created on their environment's page; there is no separate apps list or menu entry, and the apps path alone (`/releases` by default) leads to the environments. An environment's apps show each app's runtime state (why on hover or a tap), filter by it and page through 100 at a time, kept in the address (`?runtimeState=`, `?page=`). The environments pages open to either the `rel.environments` `read` setting or the apps page grant (`page:rel-apps`), so environment readers and app readers both keep an entry: the route declares no grant (`authz: 'skip'`, its menu entry shown to every signed-in user) and the page turns away anyone with neither. An environment's apps and Create App need the apps grants; the Variables tab, the connection check, managing environments and the capacity card still need the setting. Create App opens there (`<environments>/:environmentId/apps/new`, for those with `rel.apps/create`) with the environment fixed; the old `<apps>/new` address leads to the environment its `?environmentId=` names, or to the environments (`ReleasesNewAppRedirect`), and Create App no longer fails when the page under it hands no outlet context. A shared host's capacity card splits its running apps by environment (`environments[].running` in `GET /api/releases/hosts`), each linking to that environment filtered to its running and starting apps. `ReleasesAppsPage` is removed.
