Redesign the issue page around a live summary and feed shared with "My follow-ups". A summary pinned at the top of the issue page carries the title on a line of its own (the page's heading, renamed in place with its pencil), then the key, status and actions, the pull request with its checks and conflicts, the "Now" line with Start and the hand-over, what the issue needs from the viewer, and, in review, the delivery: the agent comment that tells what was delivered (cut short, with Show more) with Merge, View changes and Send back (to In progress). While the page is scrolled down, and from the start on a phone, it folds to a one-line bar with the status, the title and what waits for the viewer (Merge, Answer, or Take over and Reject on an owner handoff); one click opens it again, and "Collapse summary" folds it by hand. The body card below no longer repeats the title and status: it starts with the parent, the project, the marks, Ask agent and Delete. A star follows the issue in "My follow-ups" ("Following · n/5"; held with the reason once five are followed). The activity now runs newest first, a thread placed where its latest reply is, with "Conversation only" (the default) folding each stretch of system records (runs and changes other than status moves, pull requests and owner handoffs) into one "N system records" row that unfolds in place, and "All" showing every record; long comments are cut to a few lines with Show more and Show less, the comment shown above as the delivery notes reads as one line that unfolds in place, and the project manager's hand-overs and agents' review verdicts are tagged. Comments keep replying, editing, deleting, resolving, reactions, files and comment links, and the composer stays pinned under the activity. The side column's subscribers card is now titled "Notifications". "My follow-ups" shows the selected issue with the same summary, feed and composer, so a reply needs no trip to the issue page and comments show their screenshots as thumbnails that open the preview, and their other files as chips; its side columns stay in place while the middle scrolls, and on a phone the lists come first and choosing an issue shows it alone with a way back. Following one issue is now a request of its own, `POST /api/followUps/issues/{issueId}/follow` and `/unfollow` (`nb-studio follow-up follow <issue>` and `follow-up unfollow <issue>`, by id or identifier): following an issue already followed or unfollowing one not followed changes nothing, and a sixth is refused with `FOLLOWING_LIMIT_REACHED`; the star on both pages uses them, so two tabs no longer overwrite each other's list. `PUT /api/followUps/following` still replaces the whole list.
