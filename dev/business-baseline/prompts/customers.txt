Build customer and contact management in this clean Default application. A customer has a required name, an owner, an active/inactive status and multiple contacts. A contact has a required name and a syntactically valid email address. Include active permitted, inactive and restricted account categories for selection. Users need usable business pages and the existing user/role administration to manage explicit assignments.

An account owner can create and edit their own customers and add, edit or remove contacts on the same form. Search customers and contacts by name, filter by status, and search/filter permitted active account categories when selecting one. Save the customer and all contacts atomically: a later failed contact write must leave no partial new customer or changes to an existing customer. Reject invalid required values and email addresses without changing stored data. Keep children attached to their original customer and prevent two concurrent submissions of the same form from creating duplicate customers or contacts.

Server-side permissions must limit every list, detail, child endpoint, candidate selection and write to the appropriate owner. Other owners, signed-in users without business grants and anonymous users cannot see or change another owner's customer or contacts. Recheck selected category permissions during saves; reject or ignore forged ownership changes and attempts to attach another customer's contact. Page access and buttons must match the server rules. Include repeatable synthetic data, automated browser and server tests, and installation, startup, test and isolated-data reset instructions. No approval process is needed.
