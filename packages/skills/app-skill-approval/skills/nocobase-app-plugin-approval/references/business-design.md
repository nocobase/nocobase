# From a business request to an approval design

Write the design down before code: a process sheet, a stage table and a side-effect table. Each row is a decision the code then expresses one to one, and each blank is a question for the user or a named assumption.

## Process sheet

| Question                   | What to decide                                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Application and fact       | The application collection holding the proposal, and the business fact it creates or changes, if any; which fields are frozen once submitted                                            |
| Who starts it              | Who may create and submit, on whose behalf, and which preconditions refuse submission (budget, stock, an active duplicate)                                                              |
| The graph                  | Draft, each review state in order, correction states, execution states and final states; which record fields route between them                                                         |
| Rejection versus return    | Which stages may reject (ends the application) and which may return, to which targets, and how a corrected application resumes: from the start, or straight back to the returning stage |
| Re-entry                   | Whether earlier approvals stand after a return (`reuse: 'unchanged'` on the stages that allow it) or everyone decides again                                                             |
| Withdrawal and termination | From which states the applicant may withdraw, whether an administrator may terminate, and whether an approved application may be revoked later (a separate business transition)         |
| Held resources             | What submission reserves or locks, and on which exits it is released                                                                                                                    |
| After approval             | What approval changes or triggers, whether it reaches an external system, and what happens when that fails                                                                              |
| Who is told                | Assignee notices, applicant notices on each outcome, copies to people who never decide                                                                                                  |
| Time                       | Reminders, deadlines and escalation per stage; expiry of an idle draft or an unanswered request                                                                                         |
| Rule changes               | Whether open applications keep the rules they were submitted under (`version`, a rule stored on the record) or follow the current ones                                                  |
| Applicant recusal          | Whether each stage requires independent review or explicitly permits an eligible applicant to decide with `allowSelfApproval: true`; keep later independent reviews                     |
| Who sees and operates      | Who may read which applications, who processes, who administers (appointing, reassigning, retrying execution)                                                                           |

## Stage table

One row per review state: inclusion condition (the `route` that enters it), decision strategy, people source, missing-person behavior (`onEmpty`), qualification, offered actions with their reason rules, recipient scopes and return targets, clocks, and each outcome's exit and target. [Stages and people](stages-and-people.md) and [Actions](actions.md) give the vocabulary.

## Side-effect table

One row per step that changes something outside the approval: submit, each outcome exit, withdrawal, termination, execution and its failures. For each, write what it does, where it is declared and what undoes it. [Side effects at each step](side-effects.md) gives the places and the rules.

## Ask or assume

Ask when the answer changes money, authority or irreversibility: amount thresholds, which roles approve, whether a rejection may be returned instead, whether approval moves money or changes a master record, whether a stage may be skipped, and what happens on an external failure. Otherwise state a named assumption in the design (for example "assume the finance stage is any-one-of the finance team") and keep it in one constant so it is easy to change. Never invent organization roles or people; resolve them through the application's organization services.

## Business phrases and their designs

| The business says                                            | Design                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Example source in `packages/examples/app-plugin-approval-example/server/`          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Manager approval, N levels up                                | `stage.single` per level with `managerChain`, or one `stage.sequential` when levels share one outcome; `onEmpty` decided explicitly                                                                           | `scenarios/leave.ts`, `scenarios/contract.ts`                                      |
| Above an amount, also the CFO/CEO                            | A `route` on the previous stage's approved exit reading the frozen amount                                                                                                                                     | `scenarios/collaboration.ts` (purchase), `scenarios/contract-change.ts`            |
| Exempt below a threshold, or for a category                  | A guarded `route` past the stage; never an empty assignment                                                                                                                                                   | `scenarios/leave.ts` (compensatory leave)                                          |
| Countersignature, everyone must agree (会签)                 | `stage.all`, `onReject: 'immediate'` or `'collect'`                                                                                                                                                           | `scenarios/collaboration.ts` (countersign)                                         |
| Seats such as legal and finance, each taken by one of a team | One `stage.all` with `pools`, one pool per seat, each `claimable` or decided by its first answer                                                                                                              | `scenarios/contract.ts` (procurement)                                              |
| Any one of them (或签)                                       | `stage.any` (one approval passes; rejection only when all reject) or `stage.first` (the first answer decides either way)                                                                                      | `scenarios/collaboration.ts` (finance first/any)                                   |
| A committee vote, quorum, veto, abstention                   | `stage.threshold` with `min`, one pool per seat, `veto: true` on a veto seat; abstention through a named `abstain` action; `waitForAll: true` when every member must still answer after the result is settled | `scenarios/collaboration.ts` (committee), `action-examples/abstain.ts`             |
| A team pool, whoever takes it                                | One pool with `claimable: true`, and `claimAction`, `releaseAction`, `assignAction` with a claim `timeoutHours`                                                                                               | `scenarios/participation.ts`                                                       |
| Per-line review of an expense sheet                          | `stage.custom(itemizedPolicy, …)` with `subjects`, a partial outcome exit and `returnItem`                                                                                                                    | `scenarios/reimbursement.ts`, `action-examples/return-item.ts`                     |
| Return to the applicant, or to an earlier stage              | `returnTo({ targets })` matching the returned exit; a correction transition to resubmit; `reuse` where opinions may stand                                                                                     | `scenarios/contract.ts`                                                            |
| Hand over, add a signer, act for someone on leave            | `transfer`, `addSigner` with recipient scopes; delegation through the directory's `delegateOf` or an assignment rule                                                                                          | `scenarios/contract.ts`, `scenarios/org.ts`, `action-examples/vacation-forward.ts` |
| Ask for more material without returning                      | `askMaterialAction` paired with `provideMaterialAction`                                                                                                                                                       | `scenarios/participation.ts`                                                       |
| Copy someone in, require reading                             | `copies` with `afterEnd: [readAction]`, or `ccAction` during review; a notice that takes effect once confirmed is its own lifecycle                                                                           | `scenarios/contract.ts`, `scenarios/notice.ts`                                     |
| Remind, escalate on a deadline, let the applicant urge       | `remindAfterHours`, `dueAfterHours` with `onDue: 'escalate'`, a host sweep; `urge` as a custom run action                                                                                                     | `action-examples/urge.ts`, `action-examples/extend-due.ts`                         |
| An administrator fills a missing approver or reassigns       | `onEmpty: 'hold'`, `appointAction`, `reassignAction`, `adminRole`                                                                                                                                             | `scenarios/responsibility.ts`, `scenarios/actions.ts`                              |
| Rules change but open requests keep theirs                   | A rule version stored at submission, an explicit approval `version` bump for semantic changes                                                                                                                 | `scenarios/ruled-leave.ts`                                                         |
| Reserve a budget on submit, pay once approved                | Reservation in the submit transition, release on every exit, an execution state with an effect                                                                                                                | `scenarios/payment.ts`                                                             |
| Approve a change to a contract or master record              | A change application with a frozen `baseVersion`, applied conditionally in the approved exit                                                                                                                  | `scenarios/contract-change.ts`                                                     |
| Register or provision after approval, retry on failure       | An execution state with an effect, `onSuccess`/`onFailure`, and a guarded retry transition                                                                                                                    | `scenarios/leave.ts`, `scenarios/supplier.ts`                                      |
| Grant an authorization that is then used up or expires       | The approval creates a grant record with a lifecycle of its own in the approved exit                                                                                                                          | `scenarios/grant.ts`                                                               |
| Wait for a payment or deposit from outside                   | A ledger row per delivery, moving the record only when it waits for it                                                                                                                                        | `scenarios/order.ts`, `scenarios/supplier.ts`                                      |
| Departments review in parallel, a sub-process runs alongside | Child records with their own lifecycles and a branch row per child, concluding the parent in the child's transaction                                                                                          | `scenarios/coordination.ts`, `scenarios/work-items.ts`                             |
| One person confirms, nothing circulates                      | No approval plugin: a guarded lifecycle transition and a notification effect                                                                                                                                  | —                                                                                  |

When the applicant's case needs one more approval — overtime beyond a limit, an amount above a line — route into an extra stage by a condition on the record (the submit transition's `to` lists both stages and its `route` reads the field), not `addSigner`: adding a signer is an approver extending their own decision, and the applicant does not choose who decides.

Use the example sources when the repository is available; an installed example package ships only compiled `dist`. The tables above and the references are sufficient on their own.

## Work that is not an approval

An approval is a decision with an outcome. Do not model work that has nothing to decide as one, even when it has to show up in someone's to-do:

- Follow-up on the same application after approval — HR completing the hire, purchasing placing the order — is the application lifecycle's execution state with the transition that completes it; when a team pool takes it, a `stage.first` stage with one pool (`claimable: true`) and a custom "done" action.
- Independent work — an onboarding checklist, sub-tasks, a notice everyone confirms — is a child record per item with a lifecycle of its own (`scenarios/work-items.ts`, `scenarios/notice.ts`), not a stage whose only action is approve.

The host's to-do center lists such records as a source of its own beside approval tasks; see "The to-do center has several sources" in [Implement the approval UI](frontend.md).
